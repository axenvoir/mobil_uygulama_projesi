# Google Play Store Listing (English - Global)

## App Title (Max 30 characters)
Expira: Expiry Date Tracker

## Short Description (Max 80 characters)
Track product expiry dates, minimize food waste, and get timely smart reminders.

## Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)
Never let your food, cosmetics, or medicines expire again! **Expira** is an intuitive, privacy-first expiration date tracker and home pantry inventory manager designed to help you organize your household, save money, and reduce waste.

With Expira, everything stays safely on your device — no forced cloud accounts, no third-party tracking, and 100% offline functionality.

### 🌟 Key Features:

• **Easy Product Tracking:**
Add items with name, category, quantity, expiry date, notes, barcode/product code, and optional photos.

• **Intelligent Expiry Dashboard:**
Instantly see what is expiring today, within the next 7 days, or already overdue with clear color-coded countdown indicators.

• **Customizable Daily Reminders:**
Set your preferred daily reminder time (e.g. 09:00, 18:00) so you never forget to check your fridge or pantry.

• **Calendar Integration:**
Optionally sync expiry reminders directly into your native device calendar with a single tap.

• **Categories & Visual Insights:**
Organize products across standard categories (Dairy, Meat, Grains, Breakfast, Fruits & Veggies, Drinks, Frozen, Snacks, and more) and track your Freshness Score.

• **Smart Notes & Shopping Lists:**
Keep pinned, color-coded shopping lists and storage reminders alongside your inventory.

• **Share & PDF Export:**
Export and share your full inventory list via text, messaging apps, or generate clean PDF inventory reports.

• **Secure Backup & Restore:**
Easily backup your entire inventory data as a verified JSON file. Restore anytime with zero hassle.

• **Biometric Lock & Dark Mode:**
Keep your household data private using Fingerprint / Face Unlock, and choose between Light, Dark, or System themes.

---

### 🔒 Privacy by Design:
Your data is yours. Products, notes, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device. Expira does not collect or transmit your personal inventory to external servers.

### 🌐 Global Support:
Available in English and Turkish, with automatic system language detection following your device preferences.

# Expira Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026

Expira ("we", "our", or "the app") is a privacy-first mobile application designed to help individuals track product expiration dates and manage household inventory. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

## 1. Information Storage & Processing
- **On-Device Storage:** All products, custom notes, inventory items, user profile name, and application preferences are stored exclusively on your local device storage.
- **No Remote Servers:** We do not operate an external application server, database, or analytics tracker. Your inventory data is never sent to, stored on, or monetized by us.
- **Backups:** When you export a backup via JSON, the file is saved directly to your device or a destination of your choice.

## 2. Device Permissions
- **Camera & Photos:** Used only if you choose to take or select a photo for an inventory item. Photos remain stored locally on your device.
- **Notifications:** Used solely for local, scheduled alerts at your selected reminder time to alert you about expiring items. No remote push notifications or tracking tokens are used.
- **Calendar:** If you opt to add a product expiry reminder to your calendar, the app requests permission to create calendar events.
- **Biometrics:** If enabled, device-level authentication (Fingerprint / Face ID) is handled securely by your device's operating system. Expira never collects or stores biometric data.

## 3. Third Parties & Data Sharing
Because Expira does not collect or transmit your data, we do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties or advertising networks.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Expira does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

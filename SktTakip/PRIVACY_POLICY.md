# Expira — Privacy Policy (English)

**Last updated: 26 October 2026**

Expira is a mobile application for tracking product expiry dates, pantry inventory, notes and reminders. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Data we store on your device

All app content — products, notes, categories, settings and reminder times — is stored **locally on your device** using the device's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the backup export feature.

**Backup export:** If you use the "Backup" feature, your data is converted to JSON text and handed to the Android share sheet. Where it goes (a file, an email, a messaging app) is entirely your choice. We do not receive it.

## Data we do NOT collect

- We do **not** collect names, emails, or any personal identifiers.
- We do **not** have user accounts, logins, or servers. The app works fully offline.
- We do **not** sell or share any data.

## Crash reporting (Sentry)

The app uses [Sentry](https://sentry.io) to detect and diagnose crashes so we can fix bugs.

- If the app crashes or an error occurs, a technical **crash report** is sent to Sentry. It contains the error stack trace, app version, and device characteristics (model, Android version, locale).
- Crash reports **do not** contain your products, notes, or any content you typed.
- Sentry may set a random installation identifier to group reports from the same device. It is not linked to your identity.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** used only for the optional daily reminder you configure. Optional; the app works without it.
- **Vibration:** used for optional haptic feedback. Can be disabled in Settings.
- **Exact alarm (Android):** used only to fire the daily reminder you configured, at the correct time, when the device is idle.

No camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage permissions are used.

## Deleting your data

Everything lives on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all data. You can also use "Clear all data" in Settings within the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
